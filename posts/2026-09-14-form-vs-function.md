---
title: Form vs function
date: 2026-09-14
description: When is it okay for a thing to be a little weird?
---

Function tells you what a thing must do. Form tells you how it feels to use. We argue about them as if they compete.

The best objects I own never made me choose. They are strange in exactly the places where strangeness helps.

> Weird is fine. Weird for no reason is not.
