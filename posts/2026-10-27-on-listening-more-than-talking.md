---
title: On listening more than talking
date: 2026-10-27
description: What a quiet meeting taught me about being useful.
---

The most useful thing I did in a meeting last month was say nothing for twenty minutes. Everyone else had already said what I was going to say, and by waiting I heard the one question nobody had asked.

Listening is not the gap between your turns to speak. It is the work.

## A small practice

Before I add something, I try to repeat back what I heard. Half the time, that is the contribution.
