Hi, I'm Apekshith. Most people call me Apek.

I'm a Software Engineer at The Home Depot, where I lead the architecture for our store systems: the technology behind more than 2,000 stores and the 400,000 associates who work in them. Most days that means thinking about one person on the store floor, with a customer in front of them and a few minutes to help, and making sure the software gets out of their way.

Outside of work, I'm a husband and a dad. That's the job that teaches me the most.

## Why this site

In all honesty: Claude and Gemini :) I've wanted a place to write for years and kept finding reasons not to start. AI made getting started so easy that I finally ran out of excuses. So, here we go.

I'm not writing as an expert. I'm writing to think. Some of these notes will be half-formed, and that's on purpose.

## What I write about

- **Philosophy** -- the questions that don't have tidy answers
- **Lessons** -- things I've learned the slow way, at work and in life
- **Technology** -- building software that people actually rely on

## When I'm not working

Music, podcasts, and more interesting YouTube rabbit holes than I'd like to admit. Mostly, though, I'm trying to get better at being present: noticing an ordinary day while it's still happening.
